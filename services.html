Our Services

Project management, inspections, and construction support for owners, engineering consultants, contractors, and subcontractors.

Struvisor provides project-management, program-management, site-inspection, OSIM bridge-inspection, asset-management, construction quality-assurance, site-supervision, procurement, and contract-administration services. Services are available to infrastructure owners, engineering consultants, contractors, and subcontractors.

For projects requiring detailed structural analysis, engineering design, or specialized testing, Struvisor can coordinate qualified consultants required by the project scope.

Project and Program Management

Struvisor provides project-management services for infrastructure projects. We help infrastructure owners, engineering consultants, contractors, and subcontractors define the assignment, coordinate the right technical resources, manage site access and safety requirements, track deliverables, and ensure the project progresses in a clear and organized manner. For larger or multi-project initiatives, Struvisor can also provide program-management support, including coordination across related projects, consolidated reporting, risk and schedule oversight, and alignment with program objectives.

What we do

  • Develop project scopes, work plans, schedules, budgets, and deliverable lists
  • Coordinate engineers, inspection teams, testing providers, surveyors, and access specialists
  • Arrange site access, permits, traffic control coordination, and HSE planning
  • Organize kickoff meetings, progress meetings, technical reviews, and client communications
  • Track consultant and subcontractor deliverables, schedules, quality, and outstanding items
  • Review reports and recommendations from an owner/project-management perspective
  • Support decision-making on next steps, repairs, monitoring, further investigation, or rehabilitation planning
  • Provide program-management support for multi-project initiatives, including cross-project coordination, consolidated reporting, risk and schedule oversight, and alignment with program objectives

Site Inspections and OSIM Bridge Inspections

Struvisor provides site inspections for bridges, buildings, marine and industrial facilities, municipal infrastructure, and other structures. Struvisor also provides OSIM-based bridge inspections directly. Inspections are planned and delivered in accordance with applicable OSIM requirements, client-specific inspection procedures, and project-specific safety requirements, with clear documentation of observed conditions, defects, and recommended follow-up actions.

What we do

  • Conduct OSIM-based bridge inspections
  • Conduct or coordinate visual site inspections for bridges, buildings, marine and industrial facilities, municipal infrastructure, and other structures
  • Review asset records, drawings, previous inspection reports, and maintenance history before field work
  • Develop inspection plans, access arrangements, traffic-control coordination, and site-specific safety requirements
  • Perform visual inspections and document element condition, defects, deterioration, and observed risks
  • Prepare OSIM inspection records, photographs, condition ratings where applicable, and inspection reports
  • Prepare inspection records, photographs, defect documentation, and inspection summaries for non-bridge structures
  • Coordinate or support specialized inspection activities, including non-destructive testing, materials testing, survey, thermal imaging, and remote inspection where appropriate
  • Identify maintenance needs and recommend follow-up inspections, repairs, or further investigation
  • Support clients in prioritizing maintenance activities and integrating inspection results into asset-management processes

Asset Management

Struvisor provides asset-management services for infrastructure owners, municipalities, asset managers, and other organizations responsible for built assets. We help clients organize asset information, coordinate inspection and condition-data collection, support maintenance prioritization, and develop practical long-term capital-planning strategies.

What we do

  • Support the development and maintenance of asset inventories and asset hierarchies
  • Organize and manage inspection records, condition data, photographs, drawings, and maintenance history
  • Support condition-based maintenance prioritization and risk-informed decision-making
  • Support the development of annual, five-year, and ten-year capital plans, investment strategies, and capital-project priorities
  • Support capital-project identification, prioritization, budgeting, and business-case development
  • Support the integration of inspection findings, asset condition, risk, and operational needs into capital-planning decisions
  • Support the development of asset-management plans and lifecycle-management strategies
  • Support inspection planning, reporting, and asset-management workflows
  • Coordinate inspection teams, access systems, traffic control, and safety requirements

Construction Quality Assurance and Site Supervision

Struvisor provides construction quality-assurance support and site supervision for infrastructure and construction projects. We support owners by monitoring contractor compliance with approved quality-control plans, specifications, inspection and test plans, schedules, and project documentation requirements. We also support contractors and subcontractors that need assistance organizing, implementing, or maintaining their quality-control and site-coordination processes.

What we do

  • Provide owner-side site supervision and construction-phase project coordination
  • Review contractor quality-control plans, inspection and test plans, and project quality documentation
  • Monitor contractor compliance with approved quality-control plans, specifications, and inspection requirements
  • Attend and coordinate site inspections, hold points, and quality-related site activities
  • Coordinate quality-related inspections, documentation, and corrective actions
  • Track non-conformances, deficiencies, corrective actions, and completion requirements
  • Monitor construction progress, schedules, site safety requirements, and contractor/subcontractor activities
  • Support contractors and subcontractors with site coordination, progress documentation, subcontractor communication, and quality-record management
  • Assist contractors and subcontractors with quality-control plan development, documentation, inspection coordination, and quality-record management
  • Support project close-out, commissioning coordination, and final documentation

Construction Contract Administration

Struvisor provides administration of construction contracts in accordance with applicable CCDC documents and project-specific contract requirements, delivered as part of professional project-management services. We support owners, consultants, contractors, and subcontractors in managing contract requirements, documentation, communications, progress, changes, and project close-out in an organized and transparent manner.

What we do

  • Support the administration of construction contracts based on applicable CCDC documents and project-specific contract requirements
  • Maintain organized contract records, correspondence, meeting minutes, submittals, and project documentation
  • Coordinate contract-related communications among owners, consultants, contractors, and subcontractors
  • Track contractual obligations, deliverables, milestones, notices, and required documentation
  • Support review and processing of shop drawings, submittals, requests for information, and other contract-related documentation
  • Support progress reporting, payment documentation, and contract-milestone tracking
  • Support review and administration of change orders, change directives, claims, and contract changes
  • Support documentation of delays, disruptions, and other events that may affect schedule, cost, or contract obligations
  • Support contract close-out, deficiency resolution, commissioning coordination, and final documentation
  • Support owners with contractor performance monitoring and contract-compliance oversight
  • Support contractors and subcontractors with contract-administration processes, documentation, and coordination requirements

Emergency Response and Rehabilitation Project Management

Struvisor provides responsive project-management support when structural issues, deterioration, damage, or unexpected inspection findings require timely action. We help infrastructure owners, engineering consultants, contractors, and subcontractors coordinate investigations, procure appropriate support, and move from immediate response to a practical repair or rehabilitation plan.

What we do

  • Initial project coordination and urgency assessment support
  • Coordination of inspections, investigations, and specialist input
  • Emergency repair scoping and procurement support
  • Contractor coordination and construction oversight
  • Repair/rehabilitation project management
  • Monitoring and follow-up planning

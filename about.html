About Struvisor

Practical project management, site inspection, and construction support for infrastructure.

Struvisor Asset and Project Management Inc. is a Nova Scotia-based consulting practice providing project management, site inspections, OSIM bridge inspections, construction quality-assurance support, and owner-side construction site supervision for infrastructure and structural projects.

We support infrastructure owners, municipalities, asset managers, engineering consultants, contractors, and subcontractors who need organized project delivery, dependable field coordination, and practical support from planning through construction and close-out.

Our work spans bridges, marine and industrial facilities, buildings, municipal infrastructure, and other built assets. Struvisor helps clients manage structural evaluation projects, coordinate site inspections, administer contracts, coordinate technical resources, and deliver maintenance and rehabilitation work effectively.

We take a practical, client-focused approach: clear scopes, organized schedules, managed risks, coordinated subcontractors, controlled documentation, and transparent reporting. For projects requiring detailed structural analysis, engineering design, specialized testing, or professional engineering certification, Struvisor coordinates qualified consultants and subcontractors as required by the project scope.

Mission

To provide practical project-management, site-inspection, and construction-support services that help clients deliver infrastructure and structural projects safely, efficiently, and with confidence.

Vision

To be a trusted partner for infrastructure owners, engineering consultants, contractors, and subcontractors who need organized project delivery, dependable field coordination, and practical solutions for maintaining and improving built assets.

How We Work

Struvisor provides services from a project-management and coordination perspective. We focus on making complex projects easier to manage by defining clear objectives, coordinating the right resources, maintaining project controls, and keeping stakeholders informed.

Our approach is safety-first and delivery-focused. Site work is planned with hazard assessments, access coordination, traffic-control considerations, and appropriate safety controls. We also support clients with procurement, contract administration, quality documentation, progress reporting, and project close-out.

Whether supporting an owner, consultant, contractor, or subcontractor, Struvisor adapts its role to the needs of the project and the client’s delivery model.

Who We Serve

Struvisor works with:

  • Infrastructure owners and asset managers
  • Municipalities and public-sector organizations
  • Engineering consultants and multidisciplinary project teams
  • Contractors and subcontractors
  • Marine, industrial, and facility operators

Whether a client needs additional project-management capacity, site-inspection or OSIM bridge-inspection services, construction quality-assurance support, or site supervision, Struvisor provides practical support tailored to the project.
